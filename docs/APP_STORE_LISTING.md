### Description (4000)
```
ClipIt turns every walk into a wildlife hunt — catch and release, with a camera.

Spot an animal, snap a photo, and ClipIt's AI identifies it in seconds and scores your sighting. A pigeon on the sidewalk is worth a little. A heron catching a fish, a fox with her kits, or a hawk mid-flight is worth a lot. The rarer the animal and the more dramatic the moment, the bigger the points.

SNAP AND SCORE
• Point, shoot, and get an ID in seconds: species, a fun fact, and your score
• Rarity and scene bonuses for young, in flight, courtship, herds and hunts
• Upload photos you've already taken from your camera roll
• No signal on the trail? Captures are saved and scored once you're back online
• Think the AI got it wrong? Record your own ID on the sighting

BUILD YOUR FIELD JOURNAL
• Every sighting is saved to your personal journal with the photo, date and place
• Browse all your photos, group them by species, and search your finds
• Add your own field notes to any sighting

QUESTS, STREAKS AND THE LEADERBOARD
• Daily streaks and quests keep you heading outside
• Climb the leaderboard against everyone, or just your friends
• Track how many species you've found

SHARE WITH THE COMMUNITY
• Post your best sightings to the wall, then like, comment and follow other explorers
• Shared posts never include your location, so wildlife spots stay private
• Report or block anyone, any time; reports are reviewed within 24 hours

OBSERVE, DON'T DISTURB
ClipIt rewards the photo, never the proximity. Keep your distance, never feed or chase animals, and let wildlife stay wild. The best shots come from patience, not getting close.

ClipIt is for ages 13 and up.

Privacy Policy: https://clipitwildlife.app/legal/privacy
Terms of Use: https://clipitwildlife.app/legal/terms
```

## App Review Information (App Store Connect → version page → App Review)

**Sign-in required:** Yes. Create a dedicated reviewer account (email +
password) **with a few sightings and at least one wall post from another
account**, so the reviewer sees content to report/block.

**Notes for the reviewer:**
```
ClipIt is a wildlife photography game. Sign in with the demo account below (or Sign in with Apple).

How to try it:
1. Tap the green camera button and photograph any animal. In the office, it's easiest to point the camera at a photo of a wild animal on another screen, or use the gallery icon to upload one from Photos. The AI identifies it and awards points in a few seconds.
2. Feed tab: the community wall. On any other user's post or comment, tap "•••" to Report (reasons + details) or Block. Blocked users can be managed in Profile → Blocked accounts. Reports are reviewed by our team within 24 hours, and offensive language is filtered before posting.
3. Account deletion: Profile → Delete account (permanently deletes the account and all its data).
4. Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Help & Support are in Profile.

Notes:
- ClipIt is 13+; the sign-up age gate turns away anyone younger.
- Camera, location and photo access are requested only when used. The app works if location is denied.
- Location is stored only on the device and is never shared with other users.
- Domestic pets and people are recognized but intentionally score 0 points (only wild animals count).
- The Field Crew (Dr. Hoot, Scout, Moss, Magpie, Kestrel) are labeled bots made by our team. They live behind the bell on the Feed: species facts on each sighting, a weekly regional wildlife forecast, weekly challenges and a weekly duel. Every crew name shows a BOT badge (tap it for an explanation), and they can be hidden from the wall in Profile.
```

**Contact:** your name, phone, email.
