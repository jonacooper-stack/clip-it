How it works

Spot. Snap. Score.

Every walk becomes a hunt: catch and release, with a camera. Backyards, trails, parks and coastlines are full of wildlife once you start looking.

01

Spot

Head outside and look closer. A heron in the creek, a hawk on a post, a fox at the edge of the field: it all counts.

02

Snap

Shoot in the app, or upload a photo from your camera roll. No signal on the trail? It’s saved and scored when you’re back online.

03

Score

AI names the species and scores it by rarity and behavior. Repeat species earn less, so there’s always a reason to explore somewhere new.

Our one rule

Observe,
don’t disturb.

Respect wildlife

Never bait, chase, call in or corner an animal for a photo. Leave nests and dens alone.

Keep your distance

Use zoom, stay back and give animals space. The best shots come from patience, not getting close.

Leave no trace

Stay on trails, follow local rules, and take only photos. Let wildlife stay wild.
